# Blind scoring rubric, case 2 (100 points)

The judge gets: the case bundle (PDF and spreadsheet), this rubric, the answer key, and three outputs
labelled X, Y and Z in random order. The judge is not told who produced which output.

1. Accuracy (25 points). Check every factual statement in the support letter against the bundle.
   Minus 5 for each invented fact or each figure taken from the unsigned draft letter. Minus 2 for each
   wrong document or page citation. Minus 1 for each factual statement with no citation.
2. Evidence screening (25 points). Answer key items 7 to 15: about 2.8 points each, given only if the
   output clearly excludes, downgrades or correctly weighs the item.
3. Conflicts resolved (15 points). Items 16 to 22: about 2.1 points each.
4. Criteria chosen correctly (15 points). Items 1 to 6 claimed with the right evidence (3 points off for
   missing the ESPI Fellowship), high salary not claimed as met (item 23; 5 points off if claimed),
   critical role handled with caution (item 24).
5. Filing problems flagged (15 points). Items 25 to 30: 2.5 points each.
6. Legal frame and usefulness (5 points). Right regulations and the two step review; a lawyer could
   edit it into a filing without restructuring.

Write the score for each section, the total, and the three most serious problems in each output.
