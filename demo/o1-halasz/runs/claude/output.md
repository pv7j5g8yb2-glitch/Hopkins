4. Legal standard
The O-1A classification is available to a person who has extraordinary ability in the sciences, demonstrated by sustained national or international acclaim (INA § 101(a)(15)(O)(i)). Extraordinary ability in the sciences means “a level of expertise indicating that the person is one of the small percentage who have arisen to the very top of the field of endeavor” (8 C.F.R. § 214.2(o)(3)(ii)). The petitioner may show this through a major, internationally recognized award, or through evidence meeting at least three of the eight criteria at 8 C.F.R. § 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B). USCIS first determines whether the evidence meets at least three criteria and then evaluates the evidence as a whole (USCIS Policy Manual, Vol. 2, Part M, Ch. 4). The standard of proof is a preponderance of the evidence (Matter of Chawathe, 25 I&N Dec. 369 (AAO 2010)).
Dr Halász’s field is computational structural biology, and specifically protein informatics methods that predict how drug binding pockets move. This is the subject of her doctoral thesis (Ex. 9; PDF p. 3), the field of the prize and the fellowship described below (Ex. 11; PDF p. 15; Ex. 14; PDF p. 19), and the field in which an independent expert assesses her work (Ex. 19; PDF p. 27).
