E. Original scientific or scholarly contributions of major significance
The Beneficiary conceived FoldSight and wrote the software, according to the author-contribution statement in the 2022 peer-
reviewed FoldSight paper. That paper describes a graph neural network trained on 14,000 molecular-dynamics trajectories 
covering 2,900 proteins, designed to predict the range of shapes a protein binding pocket can take from a static structure. On 
a held-out set of 310 proteins, the paper reports recovery of 83% of cryptic pockets, compared with 41% for the prior best 
method, with a median run time of 4.2 minutes per protein. The paper states that the software was released as open source. 
(Client PDF, pp. 33-35.)
The record also includes independent expert evidence of adoption and downstream use. Professor Lucia Ferrante, a 
computational-biology professor who states that she has never worked with the Beneficiary, Corvane, or the Beneficiary’s 
former laboratory, reports using FoldSight in her own group. She states that her group and at least four other groups known 
to her use it routinely, and that several pharmaceutical companies use it. She describes the method as enabling prediction of 
binding-pocket shape changes in minutes rather than the weeks of simulation previously required. (Client PDF, p. 27.)
Dr. Mark Delaney, Vice President of Computational Chemistry at Northgate Pharma, states that his team began using 
FoldSight in 2023, before Northgate’s relationship with Corvane. He reports that the team identified a previously unknown 
binding site in an oncology target using FoldSight, that a candidate drug designed against the site entered a Phase 1 clinical 
trial in 2026, and that FoldSight has been used in three Northgate discovery programmes. He also states that Northgate’s 
2025 paid research collaboration with Corvane was partly because of this work. (Client PDF, p. 28.)
The later peer-reviewed kinome study reports a screen across all 518 human kinases, identifying 92 previously unreported 
binding sites, 17 of which were confirmed by crystallography. Its author-contribution statement attributes to the Beneficiary 
the adaptation of FoldSight for kinases and analysis of pocket-motion results. (Client PDF, pp. 36-38.)
A separate 2024 benchmark paper compares seven pocket-flexibility predictors on 1,200 approved and clinical drug targets. 
It states that FoldSight and two newer methods built on it performed best; its author-contribution statement says the 
Beneficiary designed the benchmark and wrote the paper. (Client PDF, pp. 39-40.)
The public repository snapshot printed November 1, 2026 reports 4,812 GitHub stars, 911 forks, 63 contributors, 2,140 
commits by the account “rhalasz” (71% of commits), and 182,000 package downloads in the preceding 12 months. A 
scholarly-profile snapshot printed the same date reports 1,418 citations overall, an h-index of 14, an i10-index of 16, and 612 
citations to the FoldSight paper. These are the dated figures in the packet; the Petitioner does not use the higher, 
uncorroborated figures in the Beneficiary’s email or current CV. (Client PDF, pp. 31-32; compare pp. 1, 3.)
Taken together, the Beneficiary’s documented technical leadership, adoption outside Corvane, use in external
