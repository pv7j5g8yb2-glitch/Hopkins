AUSTIN STARTUP RAISES $12 MILLION TO KEEP GRID BATTERIES ALIVE
By Priya Raman

Tidewell Labs, an Austin company that sells failure prediction software to utilities, has raised
$12 million in a Series A round led by Harbor Point Ventures, the company said on Friday.

Chief executive Sam Whitlock said the money would be used to hire engineers and expand into the
south east. The company now has 18 employees and customers in Oregon, Arizona and Georgia.

Whitlock co-founded the company in early 2024 with Lena Varga, who serves as chief technology
officer. Varga previously led battery analytics at Northfield Power Systems.

The funding comes as utilities race to add storage to handle peaks in demand. Texas alone added
more battery capacity last year than any other state.
