THE ENGINEER TEACHING BATTERIES TO ASK FOR HELP
By Marcus Hale, Senior Editor

When a battery cell inside a utility storage site starts to fail, it usually gives no warning
until it is too late. Lena Varga thinks that is the wrong way round.

Varga, the Hungarian born co-founder and CTO of Tidewell Labs, spent five years studying the tiny
drifts in capacity that appear weeks before a cell fails. Her drift model, first published in
2023, is now running at more than 60 utility battery sites across three states.

"Batteries tell you they are in trouble long before they stop," Varga said. "We just were not
listening."

Hiroshi Tanaka, vice president of grid operations at Cascade Valley Electric, said the system had
changed how his team plans maintenance. "We fix things before they break now," he said.

Industry analysts say the approach could save operators millions in avoided outages. The American
Grid Storage Council gave Varga its National Energy Innovation Prize last year.
