Volume 14, Issue 2 (2023), pages 101 to 118
A drift model for early fault prediction in grid storage
Lena Varga (Northfield Power Systems), Klaus Brandt (Danube Technical University)

Abstract: We show that small, steady drifts in measured cell capacity precede most cell failures in
stationary lithium iron phosphate systems by three to six weeks. Using data from 4,200 cells we
build a model that flags at risk cells with 91 percent precision.
