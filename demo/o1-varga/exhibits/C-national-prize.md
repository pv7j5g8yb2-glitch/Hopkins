PRESS RELEASE: Council names 2025 National Energy Innovation Prize winners

Washington, 22 October 2025. The American Grid Storage Council, the national trade association for
the energy storage industry with more than 900 member companies, announced the two winners of its
annual National Energy Innovation Prize.

This year's prize drew 340 nominations from 31 states. Nominees were judged by an independent panel
of nine experts drawn from national laboratories, universities and utilities, on originality,
measured impact in the field and adoption by industry.

Lena Varga, co-founder and CTO of Austin based Tidewell Labs, was recognised for a method that
predicts battery cell failures weeks in advance by tracking small drifts in capacity. The council
noted that the method is now used by several US utilities.

Past winners of the prize, awarded since 2011, include engineers whose work now underpins most
large storage sites in the country.
