LETTER 1
Professor Ruth Calloway, Chair, Department of Electrical Engineering, Midlands State Institute of
Technology, Ohio
10 October 2026

To USCIS,

I am a professor of electrical engineering with 25 years of research on battery reliability and
more than 140 published papers. I have never worked with Ms Varga or her company, and I know her
work only through her publications and its use in the field.

Ms Varga's 2023 drift model changed how my field thinks about battery failure. Before it, most
monitoring waited for a cell to show clear damage. Her work showed that warning signs appear weeks
earlier, and gave a practical way to detect them. I now teach the drift model in my graduate course,
and at least three research groups I know of have built on it. In my view it is one of the most
important contributions to grid storage reliability of the last decade.

LETTER 2
Hiroshi Tanaka, Vice President, Grid Operations, Cascade Valley Electric, Oregon
14 October 2026

To USCIS,

Cascade Valley Electric serves 1.1 million customers. Since 2024 we have used Tidewell's platform,
built on Ms Varga's drift model, across 41 of our battery storage sites. In the 18 months after
deployment, unplanned battery outages at those sites fell by 37 percent compared with the 18 months
before. We estimate this avoided about USD 4.3 million in repair costs and lost revenue. Our
company paid Tidewell as a customer, and I have no other relationship with Ms Varga.
