UNITED STATES PATENT APPLICATION PUBLICATION
Application number: 18/552,904
Title: Systems and methods for predicting cell failure from capacity drift
Inventors: Lena Varga, Klaus Brandt
Applicant: Tidewell Labs, Inc.
Publication date: 6 February 2025
Status: pending examination

Abstract: A method of predicting failure of a battery cell by measuring capacity over time,
estimating a drift rate, and flagging the cell when the drift rate exceeds a learned threshold.
