I. Governing O-1A standard
For O-1A classification in the sciences, business, education, or athletics, the regulations require sustained national or international acclaim and a level of expertise indicating that the beneficiary is among the small percentage at the very top of the field. 8 C.F.R. § 214.2(o)(3)(ii). A petitioner may submit evidence of a major, internationally recognized award, or evidence meeting at least three of the eight alternate evidentiary criteria. 8 C.F.R. § 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(A)–(B).
Tidewell does not rely on the National Energy Innovation Prize as the separate one-time major, internationally recognized award under § 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(A). Instead, Tidewell submits evidence under five of the eight alternate criteria: nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards; published material about Ms. Varga in a professional or major trade publication or major media; judging the work of others; original scientific or scholarly contributions of major significance; and authorship of scholarly articles. 8 C.F.R. § 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(1), (3)–(6).
