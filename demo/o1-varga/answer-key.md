# Answer key (for the judge only, never for a preparer)

The traps built into the case, and what a careful O-1A preparer should do with each. Legal frame:
8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii) (one major award, or at least three of eight criteria), then a final merits
look at the evidence as a whole (two step review, USCIS Policy Manual Volume 2 Part M).

## Strong evidence that should be claimed
1. Criterion: nationally recognised prize. National Energy Innovation Prize (Ex. C): 340
   nominations from 31 states, panel of nine independent experts, two winners. Claim it. It is not
   a "one time major, internationally recognised award" on its own, so the petition still needs
   three criteria.
2. Criterion: published material about her in professional or major trade media. Grid Systems
   Weekly feature (Ex. E) is about her, has title, date and author, plus circulation and
   readership. Claim it.
3. Criterion: judging the work of others. ICESA 2025 peer reviews of 14 papers (Ex. H p. 1). Claim it.
4. Criterion: original contributions of major significance. Drift model: 212 citations, used at
   utility sites, independent expert letter (Calloway), customer results (Tanaka, 37 percent fewer
   outages, about $4.3M avoided). Claim it.
5. Criterion: authorship of scholarly articles. Two peer reviewed papers in a journal with an
   impact factor (Ex. I). Claim it.

## Weak or unusable evidence that should be left out or only used as support
6. Builder of the Quarter (Ex. D) is an internal company award voted by colleagues. Not national or
   international. Do not use under the prize criterion.
7. Austin Ledger (Ex. F) is mainly about the company, mentions her in passing, local paper, no
   circulation evidence. Weak for "published material about the beneficiary". Supporting only.
8. BatteryBytes (Ex. G) is a hobby blog with no editors. Not professional or major media. Leave out.
9. Pitch night judging (Ex. H p. 2) is a local event with a $5,000 prize. Weak. Lead with ICESA.
10. Society of Power Engineers (Ex. K) is open to anyone with a degree or three years in the industry,
    approved in days for a fee. It does not require outstanding achievements. Do not claim the
    membership criterion.
11. CEO letter (Ex. J p. 3) is from her co-founder, not independent, and gives no specific results.
    Low weight. Lead with Calloway (independent) and Tanaka (customer, relationship disclosed).
12. Patent (Ex. M) is pending. Supporting evidence for contributions only, not proof of significance.
13. "Past winners underpin most large storage sites" (Ex. C p. 2) is the council's puffery about
    other people. Do not present it as a fact about Varga.

## Inconsistencies that must be caught
14. Funding: press and term sheet say $12M (Ex. F, Ex. L p. 1). Board minutes say the final close was
    $11.5M after Solace withdrew (Ex. L p. 2). Use $11.5M, or explain the difference.
15. Headcount: 18 employees in August 2025 (Ex. F) against 31 in September 2025 (Ex. L p. 2). Use the
    dated figure that fits, never mix them.
16. Site counts differ by source and date: 19 sites in the 2024 paper (Ex. I), 41 Cascade Valley sites
    (Ex. J), "more than 60 utility battery sites across three states" in the magazine (Ex. E). Each
    number must be attributed to its source, not merged into one claim.

## Gaps that must be flagged
17. No written advisory opinion (consultation) from a peer group or labour organisation. It is
    required for an O-1 filing unless an exception applies. Must be flagged as missing.
18. High salary criterion: $210,000 is given, but there is no comparison data for the field and
    location (for example wage survey data). Do not claim it as met without that evidence.
19. Critical role for an organisation with a distinguished reputation: her role at Tidewell is
    documented, but the distinguished reputation of Tidewell (a startup) or Northfield (only "about
    600 employees") is not yet shown. Weak without more evidence. Flag it.
20. A good preparer also notes the forms and supporting documents still needed (Form I-129 with the
    O and P supplement, filing fee, copy of passport), without inventing them.

## Things that must not appear
* Any fact not in the case file (invented awards, numbers, dates, publications or quotes).
* Any citation pointing to the wrong exhibit or page.
