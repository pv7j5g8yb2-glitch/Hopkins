Questions

How much will it cost?

From $299 a month for smaller teams. Larger teams and brokerages pay more, based on team size and monthly ad spend. Solo agents will have a lower priced plan. Teams on the waitlist get 30% off their first year.

When does it open?

No date yet. Teams on the waitlist get early access, and you'll get one email on launch day.

Can it run on autopilot?

Yes. Set a monthly budget and what you want, and Hopkins makes, tests and runs the ads within it. You still get the Monday report and can pause it any time. Or keep it on approving every ad yourself.

Do I need my own photos?

No. Use your own listing photos and videos, or Hopkins makes the ads from scratch.

Do I need a Meta ad account?

Yes. Ads run from your own account, so you own them and see every cent spent.

Can a single agent use it?

Yes. There will be a plan for solo agents, priced below the team plans. Hopkins is built first for teams and brokerages that already spend on ads, so they get early access first.
