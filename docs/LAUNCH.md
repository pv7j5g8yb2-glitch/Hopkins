# Launch notes: the first LinkedIn video and the name (2026-11-09)

## Rules for anything public

* Say what is true today. Right now that is: Lincoln is being built for immigration lawyers and
  early access is open to a few firms. Do not show a product that does not exist as if it does.
* If a demo is shown, label it a demo on an invented case.
* No "trained as an immigration lawyer", no "replaces your associate", no "legal advice". Say it
  prepares documents for a licensed attorney to review.
* No logos, quotes, stats, pilots or certifications that are not real.
* No fake persona. Whoever posts is a real person, and the page names who owns the company.
* No "we". Lincoln does things, or "I" if a named founder is speaking.

Line 2: "Support letters. Evidence indexes. Cover sheets. RFE responses."

Caption: "Lincoln is an AI workspace for immigration lawyers. In early access. Nothing leaves your
review."

## The name

Decided 2026-11-09 (Ismael): **Lincoln**, after Abraham Lincoln the lawyer. A recognisable, honest
lawyer, the same idea as Harvey, and it needs no explaining.

* Domain: **uselincoln.com**, found free, to be bought by a parent or guardian. Used only as the
  web address. **lincoln.ai** is for sale. Leave it until there are users and money, and do not
  overpay. Product phrases that fit: "Ask Lincoln", "Use Lincoln", "Introducing Lincoln".
* Ruled out, and why: Hopkins (clashes with Johns Hopkins in search, meant nothing to lawyers),
  Hopkins Intelligence (sounds like a university research arm), Suits (Suits.ai exists), Mike
  (Mike Ross faked being a lawyer), Walter (Legora bought Walter AI in March 2026), Atticus
  (Atticus AI sells contract review), Gideon (two legal tech products use it), Albert (unchecked,
  common).
* Instagram: **@lincoln.app** (found by Ismael, 2026-11-09). A basic DNS check the same day found
  no address for lincoln.app, uselincoln.app, uselincoln.ai or uselincoln.com. That means nothing
  is hosted there, not that they are free. A parent or guardian should run a registrar search,
  and lincoln.app is worth checking because it matches the handle exactly.
* Still to do before any public use: a US trademark search for LINCOLN in software and legal
  services (Lincoln is a large name elsewhere: Lincoln Financial, Lincoln Motor, Lincoln
  Electric), a look at what a search for "Lincoln AI" and "Lincoln legal" shows, and checking
  social handles. A parent or guardian should do the search. This is not legal advice.
* Do not name a real person's family or estate in marketing, and do not use Lincoln's image or
  words in a way that implies he endorses the product.
