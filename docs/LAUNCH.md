# Launch notes: the first LinkedIn video and the name (2026-11-09)

## Rules for anything public

* Say what is true today. Right now that is: Hopkins is being built for immigration lawyers and
  early access is open to a few firms. Do not show a product that does not exist as if it does.
* If a demo is shown, label it a demo on an invented case.
* No "trained as an immigration lawyer", no "replaces your associate", no "legal advice". Say it
  prepares documents for a licensed attorney to review.
* No logos, quotes, stats, pilots or certifications that are not real.
* No fake persona. Whoever posts is a real person, and the page names who owns the company.
* No "we". Hopkins does things, or "I" if a named founder is speaking.

## Video draft (30 to 40 seconds, captions on, no voice needed)

Line 1 (on screen, first 3 seconds): "Immigration lawyers lose days to the same documents."
Line 2: "Support letters. Evidence indexes. Cover sheets. RFE responses."
Line 3: "Hopkins is an AI workspace built for immigration practice."
Line 4: "Give it the case. Get back a finished Word or PDF packet, ready for your review."
Line 5: "It connects to Word, Google Docs and your case software."
Line 6: "Built for lawyers who review everything, not lawyers who skip checking."
Line 7: "Early access is open to a few immigration firms. Message to try it on a case of yours."

Shots: plain background, large serif headline, one line at a time. When the demo exists, replace
lines 4 and 5 with a real screen recording on an invented case.

Caption: "Hopkins is an AI workspace for immigration lawyers. In early access. Nothing leaves your
review."

## The name

* **Hopkins:** keep as working name. Sounds like a law firm. Search clash with Johns Hopkins.
* **Hopkins Intelligence:** sounds like a university or hospital research arm, not a tool. Not
  recommended.
* **Albert:** friendly, but a very common name. Unchecked, likely already used by other
  companies. Check trademark and domain first.
* **Mike:** do not. Mike Ross faked being a lawyer.
* **Suits:** Suits.ai already exists. Do not.
* Check before deciding: trademark search in the US (and Ireland), domain, social handles, and
  what comes up when you search the name plus "immigration" and "AI".
