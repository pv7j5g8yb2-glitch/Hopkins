# Hopkins, read as a seed investor would (2026-10-31)

Ismael asked for Hopkins to be judged from start to finish the way a partner at YC or a16z would
judge it: can this be an eight figure software company (over $10M in annual revenue) in five
years, and what has to change to get there. This is that read, then what was changed today, then
what comes next. Numbers below are public estimates or arithmetic, never Hopkins results:
Hopkins has no paying users yet.

## The verdict

**Yes, it can be, but not as "ChatGPT for real estate content".** Content generation alone is
already free (ChatGPT, Claude, Canva) or cheap (template subscriptions for agents sell at roughly
$30 to $60 a month). An agent won't pay $99 a month for words and slides they can get for free.

What can carry a $10M company is the part general assistants can't do: **running the agent's ads
under Meta's Housing rules, catching the Leads, answering them instantly, pushing them to the CRM,
and showing what each dollar brought in.** Content is the daily habit that keeps them opening the
app. Ads and Leads are the reason they pay and keep paying. Build the company around that pair.

## The maths of eight figures

* $10M a year is about $833k a month.
* At $99 (Solo) that's about 8,400 paying agents. At a blended $150 (some Duo and Team) it's
  about 5,600 accounts.
* The US alone has roughly 1.5 million NAR members and more licensees beyond that. 5,600 accounts
  is well under 1% of one country. Canada, Australia and the UAE use the same "real estate" words
  and Meta rules are similar.
* So the market is big enough. The questions are retention and distribution, not size.

## What an investor likes today

1. **A real wedge into money, not just words.** Approve and run on the agent's own Meta account,
   one Housing campaign, a page per property, Leads counted per ad. That is a workflow, not a
   prompt.
2. **The proactive loop** (Needs attention): Hopkins notices ads made and never run, Leads left
   New, money spent with nothing back, and offers one button that does exactly what it says. That
   is the start of an operator, the thing agents actually pay agencies for.
3. **Flat price, no cut of ad spend.** Easy to buy, easy to compare against an agency.
4. **Speed of building.** CRM push, instant replies, website setup, budget changes from the chat,
   carousels and reels: shipped in days.

## What an investor would push on (the honest list)

1. **No users, no revenue.** Everything above is untested with real agents. The first 10 pilots
   decide everything else. Nothing else matters as much this month.
2. **Meta has only run against a fake.** The first real campaign on a real ad account is the
   single biggest unknown. App Review, Housing category rejections, the 15 mile radius, spend
   syncing: all must be proven live.
3. **Free models.** Gemini free with Groq as backup keeps cost at zero, but answers can hang and
   quality has a ceiling. At $99 a month per agent, a paid model costs cents per agent per day.
   Switch the moment the first payment lands.
4. **Reels are animated text, not video.** Agents want property walk throughs and talking head
   clips. Animated slides are a good start for education reels; listing reels need the property
   photos moving, captions and music.
5. **No posting yet.** Downloading and posting by hand breaks the habit. Instagram publishing and
   scheduling need Meta's publishing permission and App Review.
6. **Solo founder, one channel.** Distribution today is @gethopkins reels. That works for the
   first 50 users, not 5,000.
7. **Competition will copy the content part fast.** CRMs (Lofty, kvCORE, Follow Up Boss) and
   website companies are adding AI writers. The defence is results data: cost per Lead by city,
   price band and ad idea, which only a product that runs the ads collects.

## What changed today (2026-10-31)

* **Positioning:** "The personal assistant for real estate agents" on the sign in page, the app
  and in Hopkins' own instructions. ("Realtor" is NAR's trademark, so public copy never uses it.)
* **Works like Claude:** ask for a carousel, a post, a story or an animated reel and get it in
  the chat, drawn at 1080 by 1350 (feed) or 1080 by 1920 (story and reel), with a caption.
  Download as images or a video. Say "make the headings shorter" and that one changes.
* **No ads nobody asked for.** A property on its own is read back ("Got it: 12 Elm St,
  $585,000, 3 bed 2 bath.") and Hopkins asks what to make. Ads come when asked.
* **The Brand page is gone.** Sign up is name, email and password. The brokerage name sits in
  Settings, Profile, and everything else can be said in the chat ("change my brokerage to...").
* **Free plan:** 3 creations in all (posts, carousels, stories, reels or ads, counted together),
  changes to them included, then an upgrade.
* **Today and New chat** cut to the greeting, the box and two setup rows (Meta, CRM).
* **A clean start:** every account, including Ismael's, is deleted once on this deploy. Running
  ads are paused on Meta first; if Meta refuses, nothing is deleted and it tries again on the next
  deploy. Deleting an account now also clears its creations, its Meta login and its Needs
  attention items, which it didn't before.

## The next five things, in order

1. **10 real agents, live Meta campaigns.** Recruit from @gethopkins and agent groups, run one
   listing each on Meta, and write down cost per Lead against what they pay now. This is the
   number that raises a round.
2. **Paid model once revenue starts.** Faster, better answers and reliable creations.
3. **Weekly content plan, proactively.** Every Monday Hopkins has this week's posts ready (one
   market update, one listing, one personal, one education reel) for the agent to approve. This
   is the habit that drives retention, and it fits the proactive direction.
4. **Instagram publishing and scheduling** (App Review), so approving a post posts it.
5. **Teams and brokerages.** One owner, several agents, one bill (Duo to Max Plus already price
   this). A brokerage deal is 20 to 200 seats at once, the only route to thousands of accounts
   without paid acquisition.

## The metrics to show at seed

* 50 paying accounts and about $5k a month in revenue.
* Weekly active use above 60% of paying accounts.
* Month three retention above 70%.
* A cost per Lead on Hopkins run ads that beats the agent's previous agency or boosted posts.

Hit those and Hopkins is a credible seed company. Miss the Meta proof and it is a content tool
competing with free.
