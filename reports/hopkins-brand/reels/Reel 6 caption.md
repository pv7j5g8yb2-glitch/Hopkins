# Reel 6: 7 things before your next listing ad

Cover: r6.png. Video: r6.mp4. Turn on Instagram's AI label (the voice is AI made).

Caption:

7 things to know before your next listing ad, from the basics to the one nobody tells you.

1. Housing ads go in Meta's Housing category: no age, gender or ZIP code targeting, and a 15 mile minimum radius.
2. Meta's Ad Library shows every ad running in your city, free.
3. Higher intent forms add a review step. Fewer leads, more real ones.
4. Put a call button or a booking link on the screen after they submit.
5. Name your ads properly. Every lead comes in with the ad's name.
6. One ad set with a few ads beats five starved ones. Each needs about 50 results a week to get out of learning.
7. Form leads can only be downloaded for 90 days. Connect your CRM or export them weekly.

Send this to whoever runs your ads.
